# CHIMERA Brand Rules

CHIMERA is Dior's creation and official realm network for AI characters,
roleplay, storytelling, worldbuilding, personas, lorebooks, and creator-led
fiction.

The repository may be source-available for learning, contribution, and local
development, but the CHIMERA identity is protected.

## Protected CHIMERA identity

Do not use these in a fork, public service, product, community, marketplace
listing, social account, or derivative project without written permission:

- the CHIMERA name;
- the CHIMERA logo or confusingly similar logos;
- official CHIMERA realm names;
- official CHIMERA characters, lore, and story material;
- SHARDS, VELLUM, or other official CHIMERA economy terms as a branded economy;
- official screenshots, product copy, trust language, or launch messaging;
- domains or account names that imply an unofficial service is official.

## Forks and local builds

Forks, experiments, and local builds must clearly say that they are unofficial.

Acceptable wording:

> This is an unofficial local build based on CHIMERA source-available code.
> It is not operated by Dior or Official CHIMERA.

Do not say or imply:

- "Official CHIMERA";
- "CHIMERA production";
- "CHIMERA verified";
- "CHIMERA partner";
- "the real CHIMERA";
- anything that would confuse users about who runs the service.

## Why this matters

CHIMERA is more than code. It is a product, a world, a brand, and a creative
ecosystem. These rules protect users from impersonation and protect Dior's work
while still allowing builders to learn from and contribute to the project.
