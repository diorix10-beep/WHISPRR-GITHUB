# 🔮 CHIMERA Visual Language System

## Overview
The Visual Language System defines the artistic and creative identity of CHIMERA and WHISPRR.

---

## 1. 3D Asset Guidelines (SHARDS & Collectibles)
- **Material**: High-end faceted sapphire crystalline gemstone.
- **Refraction & Transparency**: Deep sapphire royal blue & cyan internal refractions, semi-transparent crystalline structure.
- **Luminescence**: Ambient outer glow and caustics. Non-toy, non-cartoonish high-end 3D render quality.

## 2. Iconography Standards
- **Vector Library**: Lucide-React.
- **Stroke Weight**: 1.75px uniform stroke.
- **Corner Join**: Rounded (`strokeLinejoin="round"`).

## 3. Avatar Specification
- **User Profiles**: Circle (`rounded-full`)
- **AI Characters**: Squircle (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`)
- **Personas**: Rounded Hexagon / Pill (`rounded-2xl`)
- **Worlds**: Aspect Ratio (`aspect-[16/9]` or `aspect-[3/4]`)
