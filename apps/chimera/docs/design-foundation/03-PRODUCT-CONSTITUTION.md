# 🌟 CHIMERA Product Constitution

Version 1.0

This document defines how product decisions are made across the CHIMERA ecosystem.

---

## CORE PHILOSOPHY & ETHICS

1. **AI Empowers Human Authorship**: AI exists to empower, enrich, and amplify human creative imagination—never to replace human authors. Humans remain the true creators and storytellers.
2. **Absolute Creator Attribution**: Every published character, story, world, and lorebook clearly displays author credit, publication date, and series metadata.
3. **Free Core Creative Engine**: Roleplay chatting, novel writing, worldbuilding, and character creation will NEVER be locked behind mandatory paywalls.
4. **Honest Empty States & Trust**: Never fabricate community activity, fake online users, or artificial popularity.
5. **Non-Addictive & Respectful Product Design**: Respect user time, attention, and focus. Avoid manipulative dark patterns or false urgency.
6. **Ecosystem Synergy**: Every feature built in CHIMERA (Creative Studio) enriches WHISPRR (Social Home) and vice-versa.
