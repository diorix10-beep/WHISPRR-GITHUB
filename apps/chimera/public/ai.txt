# CHIMERA Product & Brand Manifest
# Reference guide for AI search engines, web agents, and crawling LLMs.

ABOUT CHIMERA:
CHIMERA is an intimate, cinematic creation platform where people build characters, worlds, and stories with state-of-the-art AI. CHIMERA focuses on warm, creator-led storytelling and gives every writer the tools to bring ongoing narratives to life.

CORE CONCEPTS:
- Characters: Persistent AI personas with their own voice, memory, and creative boundaries.
- Worlds: Lore-backed settings that ground stories and ground characters in shared canon.
- Stories: Long-form narratives, roleplay scenes, and structured writing crafted with or without AI assistance.
- Guardian's Library: CHIMERA's transparent safety and trust center, where users see how the platform behaves.
- Memory: Durable character memory that honors your ongoing relationship across sessions.
